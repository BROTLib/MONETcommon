# Emergency park on a new axis error (#86)

Date: 2026-10-20. Status: implemented in code (PR on `fix/error-park-86`), not compiled in XAE, not tested on hardware: #86 and #64 check 4 stay open until the checks in section 6 pass.
Repo: BROTLib/MONETcommon. Base: `origin/develop` after #109.

Main file: `MONETcommon/MONETcommon/FB_MonetTelescopeControl.TcPOU`.

## 1. Problem
MONET/S, 2026-10-19 (T8 step 7): an axis error during a slew drops all three drive enables and sets `bError`, then nothing
happens for 90 s (covers open, hydraulics on, `bIsParked` FALSE). A manual `park=1` runs the emergency branch correctly.
The emergency branch (`ELSIF bPark` / `IF bError`) is only entered when `bPark` is set, and nothing sets `bPark` from `bError`.

## 2. Fix
`F_ErrorParkRequest(bHwError, bHwErrorLast, bIsParked, bEstop)` = rising edge of a hardware error while not parked and no E-stop.
The member `bHwError` is `fbElevation.Error OR fbAzimuth.bError OR fbFocus.Error OR fbDerotator.bError`; `bError` is that OR `bSoftwareError`.
The request calls `Park()` right before the arbitration (after the supersede block and the horizon guard), so a park during a stop
is deferred to standstill (`bParkAfterStop`, #83/#87) and a running power-on is aborted by the park (#87). One WARN log entry.

Decisions:
- D1: hardware errors only. Software errors are command-level (0x10/0x30/0x40 timeouts, 0x15/0x16 goto window, 0x31 home below
  horizon); a rejected goto must not close the covers. An edge on the hardware part also catches an axis error that arrives while a
  software error is latched.
- D2: no automatic park while `bEstopTriggered` (people on site; safe torque off raises axis errors, the covers would start closing on release).
- D3: `SuspendAutomatic` sets `bHwErrorLast := TRUE`, so an error already present when switching back from manual does not park.
- Focus errors are included: `bError` drops all three enables on a focus error anyway.
- `bPower AND NOT bIsParked` is not used: `bPower` means "a power-on is in progress", it was FALSE in the observed case.

## 3. Interactions checked
- Stop path clears `bPark` every scan: the hook goes through `Park()` which routes to `bParkAfterStop` while `bStop`.
- After an error the drives lose their enable, `bStopped` becomes TRUE, `bStop` clears and the deferred park runs.
- Power-on from a non-parked state: aborted by the park (#87). Power-on from parked (`bIsParked` TRUE, e.g. after a PLC restart,
  the expected Wake&Shake error): no park, covers stay open.
- Loop: the emergency park ends with `bIsParked` TRUE, so the persisting error cannot trigger again until a reset and a completed power-on.
- MQTT watchdog and cabinet over-temperature already call `Park()` every scan: same path.
- Recovery after the park: `reset=1`, then `power=1` (power-on is refused while a park is running).

## 4. Tests
`F_ErrorParkRequest_Tests` (TcUnit, pure function): rising edge, persisting error, parked, falling edge, cleared and re-raised,
no loop after the park, E-stop. Stop and power-on routing reuse `Park()` (covered in `F_CommandAcceptance_Tests`) and are checked on site.

## 5. Not in scope
`bIsParked` stays TRUE for the whole power-on from parked, so a park in that window (this one or #87) closes no covers. Fixed by #113 (`2026-10-20-unpark-on-poweron-113.md`), including the Wake&Shake exemption.

## 6. On-site check (#64 check 4, T8 step 7, MONET/S, someone watching the covers)
1. Telescope online, slew to el 60 / az 120, 3 s later `MAIN.ElevationControl.bEnable := FALSE` over ADS. Expect, without any command:
   WARN 'emergency park started: axis error', brake closed, hydraulics off, covers closed and `bIsParked` TRUE after about 30 s,
   `bPark` FALSE, `bError` stays TRUE; no new park for 2 more minutes. Then `reset=1`, `power=1`: ready again.
2. Slew, `stop=1`, disable elevation within 1 s: `bParkAfterStop`, then the same park, the stop does not hang.
3. Error during a power-on that started from a non-parked state: 'power-on aborted: park requested', then the emergency park.
4. Negative: first power-on after a PLC restart with the Wake&Shake error (covers stay open); manual to automatic with an error present;
   E-stop during a move; a goto rejected with 0x15/0x16. None of them parks.
Record at 10-20 Hz: `bError`, `bPark`, `bParkAfterStop`, `bStop`, `bStopped`, `bIsParked`, `TCS_command`, park stage, hydraulics `bEnable`, covers, `READY_STATE`.
