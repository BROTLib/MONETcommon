## 1. Problem (from #121, MONET/S, 0.9.2 / BROTLib 0.9.0 / HalfBROT 0.5.6)

1. The telescope is online at el 40.
2. `track=1` below the 2 deg floor latches error 0x15 (#109, as designed).
3. `reset=1`: the error clears, `bReady` FALSE.
4. `park=1`: the log shows 'Brake is open', then 'Brake was opened without elevation axis enabled!'. Nothing visibly moved.
5. `power=1`: `bReady` TRUE after about 35 s, without a homing sweep. The elevation `bEnable` was not read.
6. `park=1`: the same warning. Elevation 40 -> 66.5 deg, then 'Elevation Axis Error: Timeout axis function block' (0x4B07).

Expected: the brake never opens while the elevation axis is disabled.

## 2. Root cause analysis

### 2.1 Confirmed from the code

**C1. The warning fires on every disable with an open brake. It does not prove that the brake was opened.**
- The scan order in MAIN is `TelescopeControl` -> `HydraulicsControl` -> `ElevationControl` (MONETS 104, 126, 135).
- When FBMTC drops `fbElevation.Enable`, `FB_ElevationControl` calls `CloseBrake()` on the `F_TRIG` (50-54). The hydraulics only
  handle `bCloseBrake` in the next scan (170-182). In that scan, `NOT bEnable AND bBrakeOpen` is TRUE, and `brakeEvent` (156) logs.
  `FB_EventLog` logs on the rising edge, so one scan is enough.
- This is also on record: in #109 check (c) (2026-10-20 14:31, the 0x15 path) and in T8 step 7, the test plan already calls the
  warning "cosmetic". It also came with the 0x4B07 in MS-19 (BROTLib#78).
- In #121 the warning is therefore expected at step 2 (the error block disables the drives, FBMTC 314-323) and at step 6 (the
  0x4B07 path, FBMTC 328-332).
- Only **'Brake is open'** (hydraulics `brakeWarning`, a rising edge of `bBrakeOpen`) is evidence that the brake really opened.

**C2. The brake closes only on an edge, and the open command is a latch.**
- `FB_HydraulicsControl.bOpenBrake` is a `VAR_INPUT` latch. It is set by `OpenBrake()` (if `bOilPressureOK`) or by a direct write,
  and cleared only by `bCloseBrake`, a pump that is not running, or a loss of pressure (170-172).
- `RESET1` also contains `NOT bBrakeClearing` and `bAzimuthLimitSwitch`, but these **do not clear the latch**. When they drop, the
  brake opens again by itself (179-182).
- `FB_ElevationControl` calls `CloseBrake()` only on the falling edge of `bEnable`. A brake that opens later, while `bEnable` stays
  FALSE, is never closed by the axis block. In automatic, the only other `CloseBrake()` callers are park stage 0 (FBMTC 1155) and
  an elevation axis error (332).
- Consequence: an open brake with a disabled elevation leaves `MC_Power` off (`Enable := bEnable AND bBrakeOpen`, 83). The
  elevation is held by nothing. This is the safety concern of #121.

**C3. Writers of `bOpenBrake` / elevation `Enable` that can act with the elevation disabled:**
- MAIN in `manual`/`off`: `HydraulicsControl.bOpenBrake := brakeClearing` every scan (MONETS 84-85, MONETN the same). After a
  switch back to automatic the latch stays TRUE (C2).
- `FB_MonetPendantControl` selection 7 with the pendant key on: `fbElevation.Enable := IbEnableButton` every scan (140).
- ADS writes, for example the T8 test wrote `ElevationControl.bEnable` over ADS.
- `FB_ElevationControl` itself: `OpenBrake()` while `bEnable` (38-48).
- `FB_PendantControl` (HalfBROT) selection 15 is not used on MONET (MAIN uses `FB_MonetPendantControl`).
- FBMTC sets elevation `Enable := TRUE` only in power-on stage 10 (1298).

**C4. Step 4: the park itself does not open the brake and cannot drive.**
- With elevation disabled, `bAxesCanMove` is FALSE (1152-1153), so the park parks in place: `CloseBrake()`, all `Enable := FALSE`,
  covers closed, hydraulics off (1154-1160, 1218-1221).
- No path of `_ParkTelescope` calls `OpenBrake()` or sets `Enable := TRUE`.
- Accepting the park while not ready is the #83/#87 design, and an emergency park depends on it. It stays.

**C5. Step 5 is expected behaviour, not a root cause.**
- `_PowerOn` stage 10 homes only axes that are `NOT Calibrated` (1304-1314). The NC keeps the calibration across a reset, so no
  sweep is the expected result.
- About 35 s fits stage 0 (the covers were closed in step 4) plus the brake delays (3 s + 1 s) and the 6 s elevation ready delay.
- The telescope `bReady` requires all three `bEnable` and `BrakeOpen` (723-728). So **at step 5 the elevation was enabled.**

**C6. A separate defect, not the cause of step 5: the elevation `bReady` ignores `bEnable`.**
- `FB_ElevationControl`: `tonReadyDelay(IN := bBrakeOpen)` (120). Elevation `Ready` turns TRUE 6 s after any brake opening, also
  while the axis is disabled.
- `_PowerOn` stage 10 uses it to start the elevation and derotator homing (1304, 1313).
- Azimuth does it correctly: `IN := fbAxis.Enable` (`FB_AzimuthControl` 23).
- Requiring `bCalibrated` (as suggested in the issue) would be **wrong**: stage 10 homes on `Ready AND NOT Calibrated`, so it would
  never home.

**C7. Step 6 most likely starts as a commanded move (BROTLib#78).**
- From `bReady` TRUE, park stage 0 takes the drive path (stage 10/20, `MoveAxis` to 60 deg). The motion was commanded by
  `MC_MoveAbsolute`, unless `bEnable` dropped between step 5 and step 6 (H6).
- Ending at 66.3-66.8 deg with 0x4B07 (`NcErr` 0) on a park from el 40-51 is the BROTLib#78 signature. #78 has five occurrences,
  also without a sweep, and MS-19 also had the warning.
- #121's "may be a different cause than #78 because no sweep" does not hold: #78 also happened from a powered, homed telescope.

### 2.2 Not identified: who opened the brake in step 4

From the code, 'Brake is open' with a disabled elevation needs one of these:

| # | Hypothesis | Evidence that settles it |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | A power-on (pyobs auto-init, `power=1`, `Init()`) ran between step 3 and step 4. Stage 10 enabled the axes and the brake opened, then the park aborted it (543-555) and parked in place. The warning came from that disable (C1). | READY_STATE 0.x between steps 3 and 4. `diagnostics/ElevationEnabled` TRUE before 'Brake is open'. The pyobs command log. |
| H2 | The manual-mode latch (C3): the cabinet was in local with clear-brakes on, then switched back to automatic. | `bOpenBrake` TRUE while `ElevationEnabled` FALSE. Brake open right after the mode change. The operator's account. |
| H3 | An ADS write (`HydraulicsControl.bOpenBrake`, `ElevationControl.bEnable`) by a test tool or XAE watch. | ADS clients connected at that time, and the operator's account. |
| H4 | The pendant key on at selection 7 (`Enable := IbEnableButton`). | `PendantControl.IbEnableKey`, `nSelection`. |
| H5 | 'Brake is open' belongs to an earlier moment (step 1-3), not to the park. | The log timestamps against the time of `park=1`. |
| H6 | (step 6) `bEnable` dropped between step 5 and step 6, so the park started with the brake open and the drive off. | `ElevationEnabled` FALSE before the motion of step 6. The order of the warning and the motion. |

H1 and H5 are the most likely: no code path is needed, only the timing. H6 contradicts C5/C7 unless something disabled the
elevation after `bReady`. **The fix in section 4 closes the brake in all six cases.** The analysis only decides whether there is a
second bug (a writer that should not exist).

## 3. Where the fix belongs, and the release chain

- **MONETcommon** gets the core fix.
  - It runs only in automatic (MAIN calls `SuspendAutomatic` otherwise). Automatic is exactly where "elevation disabled" must
    mean "brake closed".
  - In manual, the operator needs the shared Az/El brake open with the elevation disabled, to jog the azimuth (its `MC_Power`
    needs `bBrakeOpen`). The clear-brakes key does the same.
- **HalfBROT** gets two mode-independent changes: the elevation `bReady` (C6) and a debounced warning (C1).

Release chain:
1. HalfBROT 0.5.6 -> **0.5.7**.
2. MONETcommon 0.9.2 -> **0.9.3**.
   - HalfBROT pin -> 0.5.7. The library `plcproj` still says 0.5.5, `MONETcommonTests` 0.5.6.
   - `ProjectVersion`, `Global_Version`.
3. MONETS: pins (MONETcommon 0.9.3, HalfBROT 0.5.7), `ProjectVersion` bump, `Update-Libraries.ps1`, activate.
4. MONETN: the same pin bump. MAIN unchanged in both.
5. BROTLib and IAG50cm: not touched.

## 4. Options and recommendation

**Recommended: A.**

**A1 (MONETcommon): level-triggered brake close in automatic.**
- At the end of the FBMTC body, after the dispatch (so power-on stage 10 has already written `Enable := TRUE` in this scan):
  `IF F_BrakeMustClose(bElevationEnabled := fbElevation.bEnable) THEN fbBrake.CloseBrake(); END_IF`.
- This covers every case:
  - the 0x15 error path (today only a hardware error calls `CloseBrake`);
  - a brake that opens later (H1-H6);
  - the manual-mode latch: `bCloseBrake` clears `bOpenBrake`, C2.
- Calling it every scan while disabled is cheap. `bCloseBrake` is cleared in the same hydraulics call once the brake is closed (192).

**A2 (MONETcommon): extract the park decision.**
- `bAxesCanMove` (1152-1153) becomes `F_ParkAxesCanMove(...)`, a pure function with the same logic.
- It makes "the park never drives with an axis disabled or the brake closed" testable. The behaviour does not change: no new
  rejection of `park`, park in place stays.

**A3 (MONETcommon): reset.** No change. With A1, a reset in automatic with a disabled elevation leaves the brake closed.

**A4 (HalfBROT): the elevation `bReady` follows the enable.**
- `tonReadyDelay(IN := bEnable AND bBrakeOpen, ...)`, the same condition as `fbAxis.Enable`, like azimuth. No `bCalibrated` (C6).

**A5 (HalfBROT): debounce the warning.**
- `brakeEvent` triggers on a TON (500 ms) of `NOT bEnable AND bBrakeOpen`, so the one-scan overlap of a normal disable (C1) no
  longer logs. A brake that stays open with a disabled elevation still does.
- Same text, level WARN.
- In manual with clear-brakes on and the elevation disabled it still warns, as today.

**Azimuth: no change.**
- The azimuth has no brake of its own (shared `ObBrakeAzEl`) and is not driven by gravity.
- A1 keys on the elevation only: closing the shared brake when only the azimuth is disabled could brake an enabled elevation.
- HalfBROT#6 (enable against a closed brake once calibrated) stays separate.

**Rejected:**
- **B: level close inside `FB_ElevationControl`, unconditionally.** It breaks manual azimuth jogging and the clear-brakes key.
  MAIN rewrites `bOpenBrake` every scan, and the RS is reset-dominant, so the brake would stay closed.
- **B': the same, gated by a new input `bCloseBrakeWhileDisabled` that MAIN sets in automatic.** It works, but needs MAIN changes
  at both sites for the same effect as A1, so it is redundant with A1. Open question 1.
- **C: reject `park` while the elevation is disabled** (the issue's first expectation). It breaks the emergency park after an
  error or a reset (#86/#87). Park in place already keeps the brake closed (C4).
- **D: `park` enables the drives first.** It opens the brake to drive an axis that has just been reset. That is more motion, not
  less.

Size: about 10 lines in FBMTC plus two small functions, and about 5 lines in `FB_ElevationControl`. Risk: low (section 8).

## 5. Compatibility, ADS, logs

- **Public interface:** unchanged (methods, `I_Brake`, MQTT topics, READY_STATE).
- **New behaviour in automatic:** the Az/El brake is commanded closed in every scan in which the elevation is disabled. Before,
  that only happened on the falling edge, at park stage 0 and on an elevation error.
- **New behaviour in HalfBROT:** `ElevationControl.bReady` is FALSE while the axis is disabled.
  - In MONETcommon only `_PowerOn` stage 10 reads it, and stage 10 enables the axis in the same scan, so there is no timing change
    there (the 6 s delay already ran from the brake opening).
  - Check other readers in MONETS/MONETN/IAG50cm before the release (grep `ElevationControl.bReady`, `fbElevation.Ready`).
- **Log:** 'Brake was opened without elevation axis enabled!' disappears from normal disables (error, park, park in place).
  Operators and the test plan treat it as cosmetic today. From 0.9.3 on it means a real fault.
- **ADS symbols:** new `TON` in `FB_ElevationControl` (`tonBrakeWarning`), nothing removed.
- **Recording lists:** add `HydraulicsControl.bOpenBrake`, `bCloseBrake`, `ObBrakeAzEl` and `bBrakeOpen`.

## 8. Risks

1. **A1 closes the shared brake whenever the elevation is disabled in automatic.** This would only hurt an automatic sequence that
   needs the azimuth powered with the elevation disabled. None exists today: power-on stage 10 enables all three axes in the same
   scan, and every disable path disables all three. Check at review: grep for `Enable := ` in FBMTC.
2. **A1 is placed after the dispatch.** If it were placed before `_PowerOn`, stage 10 would see the brake commanded closed in the
   scan in which it enables. Harmless, because `bCloseBrake` is consumed before the next `OpenBrake()`, but it is avoided.
3. **A4 changes `ElevationControl.bReady`.** A reader outside MONETcommon that relies on "brake open = ready" changes. Check
   before the release (section 5).
4. **The warning becomes rarer (A5).** An operator who used it as the "drive was disabled" indicator loses it. The
   `diagnostics/ElevationEnabled` telemetry shows that instead.
5. **Not fixed here:** BROTLib#78 (step 6 ends at 66.5 deg with 0x4B07) and HalfBROT#6.
